environments — governance policy, deployment practice, training, and
accountability structures, written for regulated settings (NERC CIP,
FERC, NIST AI RMF alignment). Documentation-only section: the
*executable* expression of these ideas lives in
[`packages/agent-governance-kit/`](../packages/agent-governance-kit/)
and [`packages/value-registry/`](../packages/value-registry/).
